The EXoplanet Climate Infrared TElescope (EXCITE) is a NASA-funded mission that flies on a scientific balloon. It characterizes the atmospheres of exoplanets via a technique called phase-resolved spectroscopy. Here's a photo of me with the EXCITE telescope:

EXCITE carries a 0.5-meter diameter telescope and measures the spectral compisition of light from exoplant-star systems across 0.8—3.5 μm. Flying from a balloon gets you near-space observing conditions which are transparent and stable in the near-infrared.
