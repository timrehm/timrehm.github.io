My name is Tim Rehm and I grew up in Connecticut. I am a physicst, engineer, instrument scientist, and data analyst. Did college at Cornell University and received my PhD in Physics from Brown University in May 2026. My thesis was on the development and deployment of the EXCITE mission. I designed and assembled the cryogenic system, created a pipeline that detrends systematics from measured timestream signals, produced radiometric models of the instrument to quantify photon signal and noise levels, constructed pointing jitter simulations, and provided mission support of the payload during a 2024 test flight from Fort Sumner, New Mexico. Overall, it was a great time.

And it continues to be a great time! Because I now work at NASA Goddard Space Flight as a NASA Postdoctoral Program Fellow. I perform many similar tasks as I did in graduate school. We are launching EXCITE from McMurdo Station in Antarctica during Winter 2026-27, and I will need to be there to prepare the payload for launch and ultimately operate it during its flight.
